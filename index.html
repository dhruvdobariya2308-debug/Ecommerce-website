Our Story

Crafted with passion, served with soul.

Nestled in the heart of the city, Maison du Café began as a humble roastery in 1987. Today, we continue a legacy of sourcing rare single-origin beans, roasting them slowly, and presenting them in a space designed for reflection.

Our master baristas travel the world each year—visiting farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Indonesia—to bring back only the most exceptional harvests. Every cup you enjoy is a small journey.
